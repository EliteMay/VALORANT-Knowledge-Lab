# Spacing Academic Cross-check — 2026-09-19

## Research Question

VALORANTのSpacingを「固定距離」ではなくTeam coordinationの空間的要素として扱うことに、学術的な補強はあるか。

## Source

### Cultivating teamwork in esports: a framework for assessment and training

- Published: 2025-08-11
- DOI: 10.1108/TPM-10-2024-0124
- Source type: Peer-reviewed Paper
- Evidence tier: A
- Game scope: Professional Counter-Strike / general esports teamwork applicability
- URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/org/science/article/pii/S135275922500017X

## Method / Sample

- 80 professional gamers
- 16 esports teams
- Behavioral trace data
- Coordination metrics:
  - convergence
  - view coverage
  - co-location

## Relevant Finding

The study reports that the three coordination metrics were significantly related to nearly all measured offensive and defensive outcomes.

The paper therefore supports treating player positioning and spatial relations as measurable parts of coordinated team play.

## What This Does NOT Prove

This is not VALORANT-specific evidence.

Do not use this paper to claim:

- an ideal VALORANT spacing distance
- a universal meter value
- a universal trade-time threshold
- that closer co-location is always better
- that one specific crossfire geometry is optimal

## Project Interpretation

### Academic General Principle

Spatial relationships between professional players can be treated as part of team coordination rather than as an individual-positioning issue only.

### Applicability

Medium.

It strengthens the current project decision to describe Spacing functionally through connectivity to the same fight, Line of Sight, and shared-risk rather than inventing a fixed distance.

### Relationship to existing VALORANT evidence

Existing VALORANT sources already treat spacing / trading / coordinated peeks as fundamentals:

- Lothar DRX vs NS analysis
- Lothar G2 vs EDG analysis
- thwifo interview
- current trade-spacing coaching material

This paper adds an independent academic cross-check at the general esports / tactical-FPS level.

## Evidence Status

Supported as a general coordination principle.

Not sufficient by itself for a VALORANT-specific tactical rule.
